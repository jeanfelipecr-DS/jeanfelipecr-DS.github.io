/01AboutSobre

What makes a number trustworthy isn't the tool that displays it — it's the foundation underneath: who defines what the metric means, where the data came from, who owns it, whether it's complete and fresh, and which yardstick is being used. That foundation is what I build. O que faz um número ser confiável não é a ferramenta que o mostra — é a fundação por baixo dele: quem define o que a métrica significa, de onde o dado veio, quem responde por ele, se está inteiro e atualizado, e qual régua está sendo usada. É essa fundação que eu construo.

Computer Engineering (2022), Data Science training (2025), and Power BI certified. Before moving into data, I led a team and implemented ERP systems (Cigam & Nomus) in a manufacturing company — an end-to-end view of process, system and data.

Today I work in-house at a business school, owning the data stack from cloud ingestion (Cloud Run → BigQuery, raw → silver → gold) to the executive dashboards used by the board, consultants and managers. Metric contracts, a shared glossary, freshness monitoring and a single source of truth are what turned reports that didn't match into decisions nobody needs to argue about. I'm consolidating my practice in Analytics Engineering: modeling, quality, governance and delivery for decision — including the custom systems that generate the data in the first place.

Hoje atuo numa escola de negócios, dono da stack de dados — da ingestão na nuvem (Cloud Run → BigQuery, raw → silver → gold) aos dashboards executivos usados pela diretoria, consultores e gestores. An end-to-end study forecasting CPL from campaign data — feature engineering, cross-validation, model comparison. The lesson: on a small dataset, regularized linear regression beat the boosting models. Next: lead scoring via CRM join.
